# Skillsforgez Business Plan

**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared:** 29 October 2026  
**Planning horizon:** FY2027 to FY2029  
**Status:** Working plan. Commercial assumptions require validation before fundraising or commitments.

## 1. Executive summary

Skillsforgez is an AI-powered career intelligence platform that converts evidence from a person's real work, including projects, work samples, guided reflections and practice scenarios, into a living skills profile, career-direction recommendations and practical growth sprints. It addresses a core problem in career development: CVs record titles and conventional assessments capture self-perception, while neither reliably shows demonstrated capability or the most useful next step.

The product will launch with a freemium individual experience and a paid **Momentum** plan at **US$12/month**, then expand through a higher-value **Teams** offer for organisations that need a shared view of capability and development. The initial focus is career changers and ambitious professionals; teams are the scale channel once the product proves that its analysis produces trusted, useful recommendations.

Our goal is to become the evidence layer for career growth: a private, explainable record of what people can do, what they are building and where they can thrive. Over the next 18 months, the business will validate recommendation quality, paid conversion, retention and its first repeatable team-sales motion.

**Funding objective (planning assumption):** raise **US$250,000** in pre-seed capital to fund an 18-month validation runway. The capital would support product and AI engineering, privacy/security foundations, customer discovery and disciplined go-to-market experiments. This is an operating hypothesis, not a valuation or offer.

## 2. Company and mission

**Mission:** Help people discover the work they are built for and make visible progress toward it.

**Vision:** A labour market where opportunity is based more on demonstrated, evolving capability than on job titles, credentials or self-description.

**Business:** Skillsforgez is a software platform for individual professionals and people-focused teams. Its core loop is: **bring real work, reveal the skill pattern, choose a direction, forge the next skill, and return with new evidence.**

The company will operate as a privacy-first software business in **Nigeria**. Its final registered entity name and entity type should be confirmed in the incorporation records before external financing, data processing at scale or contracting with teams.

### Governance and ownership

The founding group holds **100 percent** of the business. There is no unallocated ownership at this stage. A founder-by-founder ownership schedule should be documented in the shareholder agreement before fundraising, together with any future employee option pool, adviser allocation or investor dilution. The business will have a five-member board consisting initially of the five named management-team members below. Board chair, voting rights, meeting cadence and independent-director arrangements remain to be formalised in the shareholder and board agreements.

## 3. Problem, customer and solution

### Problem

Individuals commonly face three connected frictions:

1. They cannot translate everyday work into a credible view of their strengths or transferable skills.
2. They receive generic career and learning advice that does not fit their goal, time or evidence of capability.
3. They struggle to show growth in interviews, performance discussions and career changes.

Teams experience the same issue at an organisational level: talent conversations often rely on titles, manager opinion and incomplete self-assessment, making capability gaps and internal mobility hard to see.

### Solution

Skillsforgez analyses user-provided work evidence to identify capabilities such as reasoning, communication, execution and collaboration. It provides:

| Product element | Customer outcome |
|---|---|
| Work-signal analysis | Turns projects, tasks and reflections into skill evidence. |
| Living skills profile | Shows demonstrated strengths, emerging capabilities and development edges. |
| Career-direction engine | Explains role and environment fit using current capability. |
| Personalised growth sprints | Converts insight into short, practical actions and reflection. |
| Progress narrative | Gives users a clear account of what they practised and can now do. |
| Team skill landscape (Teams) | Gives managers a shared, consent-based view of capability and pathways. |

### Initial ideal customer profiles

**Primary B2C:** Nigerian professionals and aspiring professionals aged **18 to 40** in the information-technology sector. They have meaningful work examples but lack confidence or clarity about their next move. They are motivated by a job change, promotion, performance review or deliberate upskilling.

**Primary B2B:** Nigerian people and learning leaders in small and mid-sized IT and knowledge-work teams of 50 to 500 employees, with a visible need for internal mobility, capability development or more useful manager-development conversations.

### Launch geography

Nigeria is the initial launch country. The early product and marketing experience should be designed for Nigerian IT professionals and employers, including locally relevant career pathways, pricing/payment preferences and trusted community channels. International expansion should follow only after the Nigerian launch demonstrates retention and a repeatable acquisition motion.

## 4. Market opportunity and positioning

Skillsforgez operates at the intersection of career coaching, skills assessment, learning and talent intelligence. Its entry market is not "everyone seeking work"; it is users with enough work evidence to benefit from an evidence-led profile and a near-term decision to make.

### Positioning

> **Skillsforgez helps people turn real work into an explainable skills story and a practical next move.**

The differentiation is not a generic AI chat experience or a one-time personality test. It is the evidence-to-action loop: users bring work, receive a transparent interpretation, act on a focused sprint and return with new proof.

### Competitive alternatives

| Alternative | Strength | Skillsforgez response |
|---|---|---|
| CV builders and job boards | Discovery and application workflow | Focus on capability discovery before the application. |
| Personality/skills assessments | Simple benchmark and language | Use real work evidence and update the profile over time. |
| Career coaches | Human empathy and accountability | Offer accessible, always-on guidance; partner with coaches where human support is needed. |
| Learning platforms | Broad content libraries | Start with a specific capability goal and recommend action, not a catalogue. |
| Talent-management suites | Enterprise workflow and data integrations | Begin with a lighter, employee-centred product and prove utility in small teams. |

### Market-sizing method to validate

Do not present an unsupported top-down TAM as traction. Instead, calculate opportunity from reachable segments:

* **B2C serviceable market:** qualified audience reached through content, partnerships and communities multiplied by free-profile activation, paid conversion and annual recurring revenue per paid user (US$144 at current price).
* **B2B serviceable market:** target organisations in the first geography or vertical multiplied by qualified sales conversations, pilot win rate and annual contract value.

The first 90 days should establish the numbers from real acquisition channels, interviews and pilots. The plan deliberately avoids claiming a market-size figure until that work is complete. Its first market-sizing exercise should calculate the number of reachable Nigerian IT professionals aged 18 to 40 and the number of target Nigerian IT employers, then apply observed conversion and contract rates.

## 5. Business model

### Revenue model

| Plan | Published offer | Revenue logic |
|---|---|---|
| Explorer | Free foundational profile, one guided reflection and three tailored next steps | Acquisition, activation and trust-building. |
| Momentum | US$12/month; unlimited reflections, personalised sprints and progress trends | Recurring B2C subscription revenue. |
| Teams | Skill landscape, shared pathways, manager insight dashboard and onboarding | Annual B2B subscription priced by team size and support needs. |

**Pricing actions:** retain the US$12/month test price for the first cohort; test annual billing and a higher-value tier only after observing activation and retention. For Teams, price pilots explicitly, then convert successful pilots to annual contracts. Avoid custom feature work that does not strengthen the common product.

### Unit-economics targets (to validate)

| Metric | Initial operating target | Why it matters |
|---|---:|---|
| Profile completion after sign-up | At least 60 percent | Measures whether first value is clear. |
| Free-to-paid conversion | At least 5 percent by month 6 | Tests willingness to pay. |
| Month-3 paid retention | At least 70 percent | Tests ongoing product value. |
| B2C customer-acquisition cost | No more than US$35 blended | Supports a payback period under three months at US$12/month before gross margin. |
| B2B pilot-to-annual conversion | At least 40 percent | Validates the team proposition. |
| Gross margin | At least 80 percent | Requires disciplined AI/inference and support costs. |

## 6. Go-to-market plan

### Stage 1: prove individual value (months 0 to 6)

Recruit a design-partner cohort of 75 to 150 individuals through career-change communities, professional groups, alumni networks and founder-led outreach. Offer concierge onboarding in exchange for structured feedback. The conversion event is not merely sign-up; it is completing a profile, understanding one recommendation and taking one action.

Content should demonstrate the product's point of view: how to convert a project into skills evidence, prepare a development conversation, or identify transferable strengths. Use case studies only with permission and clearly distinguish real outcomes from illustrative demos.

### Stage 2: establish a repeatable B2C channel (months 6 to 12)

Double down on channels with measurable activated-user cost: search/content, trusted community partners, coach/referral partners and webinars. Build email lifecycle prompts around the next growth sprint and new work evidence. Paid acquisition begins only after retention supports it.

### Stage 3: sell accountable team pilots (months 9 to 18)

Approach 20 to 30 people and learning leaders in a narrow vertical. Sell 8 to 12 week pilots with a mutually agreed outcome: employee activation, manager-usefulness score, skill evidence added, internal-mobility conversation quality or development-plan completion. Convert the strongest pilots into annual subscriptions and publish anonymised proof points.

### Sales funnel

`Audience, then skills-profile sign-up, then completed first reflection, then recommendation understood, then paid Momentum or team pilot, then retained subscriber or annual team contract`

## 7. Product, technology and operations

### Product roadmap

| Period | Deliverables | Decision gate |
|---|---|---|
| Months 0 to 3 | Reliable intake, profile explanation, feedback capture, consent controls | Do users understand and trust the analysis? |
| Months 4 to 6 | Growth-sprint workflow, progress narrative, subscription billing, analytics | Are users returning and converting? |
| Months 7 to 12 | Improved recommendation quality, coach and partner workflow, pilot-ready team controls | Can a team pilot deliver a measurable result? |
| Months 13 to 18 | Team dashboard, administration and security maturity, annual-contract workflow | Is the B2B motion repeatable and retention-positive? |

### Responsible AI and data commitments

The business handles career-relevant information and may influence consequential user choices. It must therefore:

* analyse only material the user has the right to share and make the user the source of consent;
* explain recommendations as evidence and confidence, not as definitive judgments;
* provide correction, deletion, export and opt-out paths;
* minimise stored personal data, separate identity from analysis where practical, and set retention limits;
* test for uneven performance across relevant groups and prohibit discriminatory employer use;
* use human review and escalation for high-impact team use cases; and
* maintain a documented model, data and vendor-risk review before enterprise sales.

These safeguards are product requirements, not a later compliance project.

It is a budgeting tool, not a forecast or statement of current performance. Skillsforgez is pursuing a revenue-driven model, but it has not yet reported revenue, a waitlist or quantified paid traction.

Current market learning is based on news and podcast research, human conversations and observed high engagement with the concept. The business reports few direct competitors in its initial niche. These are early qualitative signals, not statistically validated market evidence. There is currently **no waitlist**, and the plan does not claim existing revenue or quantified engagement metrics. ## 10. Risks, context and responses

| Risk/context change | Early signal | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Users do not trust AI interpretation | Low profile completion; negative qualitative feedback | Show source evidence, uncertainty and edit/correct mechanisms; use human-reviewed evaluation. |
| Weak retention | Paid users stop after first insight | Improve sprint accountability and recurring value before increasing acquisition spend. |
| AI costs grow faster than revenue | Cost per active user rises | Measure cost by workflow, set limits, cache/reuse safely and redesign expensive flows. |
| Privacy or employment-law exposure | Teams request opaque scoring or employee surveillance | Refuse prohibited uses; maintain consent, data-minimisation and legal review. |
| Large platforms copy surface features | Prospects see it as a feature | Defend with trusted workflow, evidence quality, user relationships and vertical proof. |
| B2B sales cycles are slow | Pilots stall or require excessive tailoring | Keep B2C learning engine active, sell narrow pilots and productise common needs. |
| Founder/key-person dependency | Decisions or sales stop when one person is unavailable | Document operating processes, add advisers and hire complementary capability. |

## 11. Risk and reward scenarios

In the Sahlman framework, the plan should show how the venture adapts rather than pretend the future is certain.

| Scenario | Conditions | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Downside | Conversion <3%, month-3 retention <50%, no paid team pilot by month 12 | Pause paid acquisition, narrow to the best-performing individual use case, reduce burn and re-test willingness to pay. |
| Base case | Targets in Section 8 achieved; early team pilots convert | Continue B2C growth and productise the team workflow. |
| Upside | Strong referral growth, retention at least 80 percent and rapid pilot conversion | Add sales capacity carefully, prioritise security and customer success before broad enterprise expansion. |

## 13. Planning framework and sources

This plan follows the conventional startup-plan sequence of executive summary, problem and solution, market, product, marketing, operations, financials and appendix, while applying William A. Sahlman's four investor questions: **people, opportunity, context, and risk and reward**. The factual product and pricing inputs are drawn from the supplied Skillsforgez website files. The financials, milestones, hiring, funding and market assumptions are newly created planning assumptions, not claims of existing performance.

Sahlman's central point is that a plan should be a road map for adapting to uncertainty, not a falsely precise prediction. See Harvard Business School's summary of the framework: [How to Write a Great Business Plan](https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/how-to-write-a-great-business-plan).
