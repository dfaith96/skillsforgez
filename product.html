The Skillsforz product

Your work is data.
Make it useful.

Skillsforz is an AI-powered career intelligence platform that observes real work, identifies demonstrated skills and turns them into a personal plan for growth.

Why Skillsforz exists

People are more
than a CV.

A CV records titles. Traditional assessments capture self-perception. Skillsforz looks at the evidence in between: the decisions you make, the problems you untangle, the way you communicate and the work you bring to life. This creates a living skills profile that is more accurate, more human and far more useful.

Everything in one place

From hidden ability to
visible next steps.

01

Work signal analysis

Share a project, portfolio, task reflection or practice scenario. Our AI reads for the capabilities behind the output, including reasoning, communication, execution and collaboration.

02

Skills profile

See your strongest demonstrated skills, emerging capabilities and development edges in one clear profile that updates as your work grows.

03

Career direction engine

Explore career paths that fit your real strengths. Understand why each path matches, what it requires and where you already have an advantage.

04

Personalised growth sprints

Get focused learning plans built around one practical goal at a time. Every sprint includes actions, reflection prompts and proof you can carry forward.

05

Progress you can explain

Skillsforz turns invisible development into a simple story: what you practised, what changed and what you can now do with confidence. Use it to prepare for interviews, performance reviews or your next internal move.

How the product works

A repeatable growth loop.

STEP 01

Bring real work

Add a completed project, answer a work-style prompt or take on a guided challenge. You decide what evidence represents you best.

STEP 02

See the pattern

Skillsforz translates your evidence into a skills signal: strengths you are already using, gaps that are holding you back and potential you can build on.

STEP 03

Choose a direction

Compare career possibilities through the lens of your current capability, not just job titles. Find the roles and environments where you are likely to thrive.

STEP 04

Forge the next skill

Follow a short, personalised sprint that makes progress manageable. Return with new evidence and the profile becomes sharper again.
