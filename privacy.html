Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Skillsforz respects your privacy. This policy explains the information we collect, how we use it and the choices available to you when you use our website and services.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, contact details, career goals, work samples and responses to Skillsforz activities. We may also collect basic technical information, such as device and usage data, to operate the service.

2. How we use information

We use your information to provide skills analysis, create personalised learning recommendations, respond to your enquiries, maintain security and improve the Skillsforz experience. We only process work evidence for the purposes described in this policy and the service you request.

3. Your work and privacy

Your submitted work remains yours. We do not sell your personal information or work samples. We use appropriate safeguards to protect information in our care and limit access to people and systems that need it to deliver the service.

4. Sharing information

We may use trusted service providers to help operate Skillsforz. They may process information only on our instructions and with appropriate protections. We may also share information when required by law or to protect the safety and integrity of our users and service.

5. Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements. You can also contact us with questions about how your data is used.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@skillsforz.com.
